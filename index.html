Who We Are

A nonprofit built on the belief that reentry is a community responsibility.

Reborn Ready Corp is a Macon-based nonprofit organization dedicated to reducing recidivism through a comprehensive, integrated approach to reentry. We don't offer isolated services — we build systems of support that address housing, employment, and wellbeing together.

As the mission holder and grant applicant for the Reborn Ready ecosystem, we oversee program design, fund development, community partnerships, and outcome tracking. Our work is grounded in Macon-Bibb County and designed to meet people where they are — on the day they come home.

We serve justice-involved adults at moderate-to-high recidivism risk, with specialized tracks for veterans and individuals in recovery from substance use disorders.

Meet the Founder

Crystal "Chrissy" Hunt

Founder & CEO, Reborn Ready Corp

Chrissy Hunt is a Macon-based nonprofit leader, entrepreneur, and community advocate with over seven years of experience in criminal justice administration and a degree in Criminal Justice. Before founding Reborn Ready Corp, she served in administrative and program coordination roles at a police department — work that gave her a front-row seat to the cycle of incarceration and the gaps in support that too often lead people back into the system.

But Chrissy's understanding of instability goes beyond the professional. She has experienced housing insecurity firsthand — a chapter of her life that gave her something no degree or job title could: the lived knowledge of what it feels like to have nowhere to go, and the unshakeable conviction that no one should face that alone.

That experience didn't break her. It built her purpose.

In response, Chrissy built Reborn Ready from the ground up — an integrated reentry ecosystem designed to address the root causes of recidivism through stable housing, meaningful employment, and wraparound support. Her vision goes beyond services: she believes that when justice-involved adults are given real tools and real opportunities, they don't just survive reentry — they become contributors to the communities they return to.

Chrissy leads Reborn Ready Corp as its Founder and CEO, overseeing mission strategy, grant development, and community partnerships across Macon-Bibb County.

Needs Statement

"Georgia holds the seventh-highest incarceration rate in the nation, yet only 15% of people released from state prisons pass through structured step-down housing. The remaining 85% reenter Macon-Bibb County with no formal housing plan, at a time when over 10% of returning citizens nationally become homeless within months of release. Reborn Ready Corp addresses this gap directly, pairing stable housing with workforce development to address the two most cited drivers of recidivism: lack of residence and lack of income."

What We Do

Programs built around the whole person.

📋

Case Management

Individualized support plans connecting clients to housing, employment, benefits, counseling, and community resources — coordinated by trained case managers.

🏛️

Reentry Navigation

Guidance through the practical challenges of reentry: ID restoration, benefits enrollment, court obligations, transportation, and more.

🔧

Workforce Development

Earn-while-you-learn skilled trades training in partnership with GaChi Handyman Services. Clients build a résumé, earn income, and develop real-world skills simultaneously.

🎖️

Veterans Reentry Track

A dedicated program for justice-involved veterans offering housing priority, VA benefits navigation, peer support, and connection to veteran-specific employment opportunities.

🤝

Community Partnerships

We work alongside Macon-Bibb County agencies, faith organizations, employers, and courts to create a coordinated reentry safety net for returning residents.

📈

Recovery Support Track

Coming Year 2: a dedicated track for individuals in recovery from substance use disorders, combining housing stability with peer recovery coaching and treatment linkage.

Reborn Ready Corporation Launches Comprehensive Transitional Housing Initiative in Macon to Combat Homelessness and Recidivism

MACON, GA — Reborn Ready Corporation, a newly established 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, is proud to announce its official launch in Macon, Georgia. Guided by the motto, "Where Completion Opens the Door to Rebirth," the organization introduces a holistic, evidence-aligned model designed to support adults transitioning from homelessness, recovery programs, and incarceration.

The organization addresses systemic barriers through a rigorous, accountability-based program built on five core pillars: Structured Housing, Accountability & Skill Building, Transportation Support, Resource Navigation, and Community Integration — including a dedicated track for veterans.

For its inaugural year, Reborn Ready has set ambitious goals: serving 40–60 individuals, targeting a 65–75% housing retention rate after six months, and securing job placements for at least 35–50% of participants within three months of entry.
