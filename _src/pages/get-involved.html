Where it goes

What your money actually buys

No fancy math. Just the real stuff we're shopping for right now.

Day one

A move-in kit

Sheets, towel, pillow, hygiene stuff, a phone charger. Most people show up with a bag. Sometimes not even that.

Week one

Getting around

Bus passes and rides to supervision, interviews, and the DMV. One missed appointment can cost someone everything.

Month one

Work gear

Steel-toe boots, gloves, basic tools, and work clothes so residents can start on GaChi job sites right away.

The big one

The first house

Deposit, rent, utilities, and furniture for our first leased residence. This is where seed funding matters most.

Other ways in

Not a donor? You still have something we need.

Funders & grantmakers

Foundations, government programs, faith funds, and impact investors. We'll send our program overview, budget, and IRS determination letter. Ask hard questions. We'd rather you did.

Request our program overview

Referral partners

Probation and parole officers, courts, accountability courts, chaplains, treatment programs, and shelters. If you work with people coming home to the Macon area, let's set up a referral path.

Start a referral relationship

Employers

Hire one of our residents. They'll show up with transportation figured out, a case manager in their corner, and somebody checking in. That's more support than most new hires get.

Talk hiring

Landlords & property owners

We lease homes for our residents, and Reborn Ready Housing LLC signs the lease. If you've got a house in Macon, Jones County, or nearby that would work as shared housing, call us.

Offer a property

Volunteers

Teach a workshop on budgeting, resumes, cooking on a budget, or getting a license back. Drive someone to an interview. Help paint a house before move-in day.

Volunteer

In-kind donations

Twin bed frames, mattresses, dressers, kitchen basics, new bedding, hygiene items, and gently used work boots. Call first so we can make sure we've got room.

Spread the word

Tell somebody about us

Honestly, this might be the biggest help of all. Share our page, tag us, send the flyer to your church group or your job. Somebody you know either needs us or knows somebody who does.
