Our Programs

Structured housing and support for veterans and men in reentry

Here's what living with Reborn Ready actually looks like, the support and the expectations. Serving Middle Georgia.

Housing is currently full. We don't want to lose you, though. Leave us a message with your details and we'll add you to the waitlist and reach out when a bed opens up.

What we provide

Eight services, one plan

Every resident gets all of it. They overlap on purpose. A job lead means nothing if you can't keep your bed, and a bed means nothing if you never leave the house.

1

Structured transitional housing placement

A real house, with real rules

A spot in a shared, supervised home in Middle Georgia. The address stays private to protect residents. Not a shelter cot. A room, a kitchen, a front door, and people around you who are working toward the same thing.

2

Daily accountability and house expectations

Everybody knows the deal

Curfew, chores, check-ins, respect for the house and the people in it. Some folks hear "rules" and think it's prison again. It's not. Structure is what lets you breathe, because nobody's guessing what's expected.

3

Reentry guidance and support

The paperwork nobody warns you about

Getting your ID, Social Security card, and birth certificate back. Staying on top of parole requirements if you've got them. Figuring out benefits. We walk through it with you.

We also have a pending agreement with the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS) to become an Indigent Resource Provider. Once that's final, we'll be able to give qualifying residents a voucher for a reduced-fee Georgia ID card, the way DDS's Indigent ID program works.

4

Life-skills coaching

Stuff nobody teaches you inside

  • Budgeting and actually saving a paycheck
  • Cooking, cleaning, and running a household
  • Handling conflict, because shared houses get tense
5

Employment readiness support

Getting you hired and keeping you hired

Resumes, applications, interview prep, what to say about your background, and how to show up on day one. When there's a fit, residents can also get hands-on experience with our partner GaChi Handyman Services.

6

Community resource referrals

Connected to the right people

We can't do everything, and we won't pretend to. We connect residents to VA services, mental health counseling, addiction recovery, healthcare, job placement, and other local help across Middle Georgia.

7

Goal tracking and personal development

Seeing yourself move

You set goals when you move in: ID, job, savings, a place of your own. We check them regularly. Some weeks you'll be ahead, some weeks behind. Either way you'll know where you stand.

8

Stable program oversight for residents

Somebody's always paying attention

Consistent staff oversight of every house and every resident. Problems get caught early, before they turn into somebody losing their spot.

For veterans

You served. That still counts.

Maybe you came home and the job fell through. Maybe the housing did. Maybe you got jammed up with the law along the way. None of that erases the fact that you served.

Veterans get the same house and structure as everyone else, plus help connecting with VA benefits and veteran services (which, let's be honest, can be a full-time job by itself).

Who we serve

Who can apply

You have to want the structure and be ready to work for it. That's the main thing.

  • Veterans
  • Nonviolent reentry: men on parole, or men whose parole has already ended
  • Men facing homelessness and unemployment

Right now our houses are for men only. We plan to expand.

Being straight with you: we only serve people with nonviolent backgrounds. That's not a judgment on anybody. It's what we can handle safely in a shared house.

How the waitlist works

  1. You, a family member, a case manager, or a parole officer sends us a message or calls.
  2. Tell us your name, how to reach you, whether you're a veteran, and when you need housing.
  3. We add you to the waitlist. Housing is full right now, so this part takes patience.
  4. When a bed opens, we call you for a screening and go over the house expectations together.
