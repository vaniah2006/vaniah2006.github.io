October 2026 · Update

Housing full, waitlist open, and a pending agreement with Georgia DDS

Our beds are full. If you or someone you know needs housing, leave us a message with your details and we'll add you to the waitlist for future contact.

We serve veterans, men in nonviolent reentry (parolees and men whose parole has ended), and men facing homelessness and unemployment, all on equal footing. Right now our houses are for men only; we plan to expand. We serve Middle Georgia.

Reborn Ready Corporation also has a pending agreement with the Georgia Department of Driver Services (DDS) to serve as an Indigent Resource Provider. Once it's approved, we'll be able to help qualifying residents get a reduced-fee Georgia ID card through DDS's Indigent ID program.

July 31, 2026 · Press release · ABNewswire

Reborn Ready Corporation Launches Comprehensive Transitional Housing Initiative in Macon to Combat Homelessness and Recidivism

MACON, GA. Reborn Ready Corporation, a newly established 501(c)(3) nonprofit, announced its official launch in Macon, Georgia. Under the motto "Where Completion Opens the Door to Rebirth," the organization supports adults transitioning out of homelessness, recovery programs, and incarceration.

The program is built on five core pillars: Structured Housing, Accountability & Skill Building, Transportation Support, Resource Navigation, and Community Integration, which includes a dedicated track for veterans.

For its first year, Reborn Ready set three goals: serve 40 to 60 people, reach a 65 to 75 percent housing retention rate after six months, and help at least 35 to 50 percent of participants land a job within three months of entry.

The organization is seeking seed funding to cover its first twelve months of operations and capital for its first residence, and is inviting local employers, behavioral health providers, and faith-based groups to set up referral and employment pathways.
