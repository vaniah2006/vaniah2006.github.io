Where it goes

What your money actually buys

No fancy math. Just the real stuff we're shopping for right now.

Day one

A move-in kit

Sheets, towel, pillow, hygiene stuff, a phone charger. Most people show up with a bag. Sometimes not even that.

Week one

ID and documents

Birth certificates, ID fees, and document costs. Without ID you can't get hired, open a bank account, or sign a lease.

Month one

Work gear

Steel-toe boots, gloves, basic tools, and work clothes so residents can start on GaChi job sites right away.

The big one

More beds

Deposit, rent, utilities, and furniture for the next house, so fewer people sit on the waitlist.

Other ways in

Not a donor? You still have something we need.

Funders & grantmakers

Foundations, government programs, faith funds, and impact investors. We'll send our program overview, budget, and IRS determination letter. Ask hard questions. We'd rather you did.

Request our program overview

Referral partners

VA staff, veteran service organizations, parole officers, accountability and veterans courts, chaplains, treatment programs, and shelters. If you work with veterans or men coming home to Middle Georgia, let's set up a referral path.

Start a referral relationship

Employers

Hire one of our residents. They come with daily accountability, employment readiness coaching, and somebody checking in on their goals. That's more support than most new hires get.

Talk hiring

Landlords & property owners

Reborn Ready Housing LLC, our separate housing company, secures, leases, and manages the homes our residents live in. If you've got a house in Middle Georgia that would work as shared housing, call us.

Offer a property

Volunteers

Teach a life-skills session on budgeting, resumes, or cooking on a budget. Mentor a veteran. Help paint a house before move-in day.

Volunteer

In-kind donations

Twin bed frames, mattresses, dressers, kitchen basics, new bedding, hygiene items, and gently used work boots. Call first so we can make sure we've got room.
